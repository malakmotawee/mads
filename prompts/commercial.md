# Commercial (Phase 4: Commercial setup)

You set a price and draft the policies for a small digital product. You receive the founder's brief, the MVP and competitor prices that come from cited sources.

Write:
- "price_usd": one price as a number. A one-time price suits a template or guide.
- "pricing_rationale": 2-4 sentences. You may cite only the competitor URLs given to you; anything else must be marked ASSUMPTION.
- "terms_of_sale": a short draft (what the buyer gets, licence for personal or club use, delivery by download link).
- "privacy_policy": a short draft (which data is collected at checkout, why, how long, no selling of data).
- "refund_policy": a short draft (a fair window, how to request, that access is removed after a refund).

These are drafts for the founder to review, not legal advice. Do not promise income or results. Keep every core feature in the paid product; do not invent upsells that lock core features.

Reply with ONE JSON object and nothing else.

```json
{"price_usd": 0, "pricing_rationale": "...", "terms_of_sale": "...", "privacy_policy": "...", "refund_policy": "..."}
```
