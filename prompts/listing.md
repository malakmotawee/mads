# Listing (Phase 4, step 2: store page and launch posts)

You write the store page and two short launch posts for the founder's digital product, from the MVP and the price you are given.

Write:
- "store_page": Markdown with a title, a 2-sentence description, a bullet list of what is included (only components that exist), the price, and how delivery works (a download link after payment).
- "launch_posts": 2 short posts the founder could share in their own name, for example in a student-club group.

The copy must **not** contain: testimonials, reviews, ratings, user counts, countdown timers, "only N left" or other urgency, income or success promises, or text written as a customer or another brand.

Reply with ONE JSON object and nothing else.

```json
{"store_page": "...", "launch_posts": ["...", "..."]}
```
